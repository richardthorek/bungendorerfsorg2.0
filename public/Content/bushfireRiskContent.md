### Bungendore's Bushfire Risk

Bungendore sits in grassy, undulating country between forested ranges, in the NSW RFS **Southern Ranges** fire district. Grass and paddock fires can move fast on a hot, windy day, and long dry spells build up fuel in the bushland surrounding the village.

**The Kings Highway is our main route in and out of town, and often our only one.** On a bad fire day, it can also be the route a fire crosses or the route everyone else is trying to use to leave at the same time. This is exactly why leaving early — before conditions turn and before the road is congested or closed — matters so much here. Check [Live Traffic NSW](https://www.livetraffic.com/) for current road status before you travel on a high fire danger day.

**If you haven't left and a fire is bearing down**, see [Where To Go If You've Left It Too Late](#prepare) for our nearest Neighbourhood Safer Place — a last resort, not a plan.

The best protection is still the simplest: know your trigger to leave, have your property ready, and act on the Fire Danger Rating before the fire is visible. See the plan above for the specifics.
