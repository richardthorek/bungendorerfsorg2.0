### Join the Bungendore RFS: Make a Difference!

**Become part of an active and dedicated team.** Most months we train on the second Saturday morning and fourth Tuesday evening, plus specialized sessions throughout the year to keep your skills sharp.

**Your Journey to Firefighter:** Qualification can take up to 12 months, depending on course availability and assessment schedules. We're with you every step of the way.

**Curious? Drop By and Say Hello!**
Prospective members are welcome to drop in and say hello during our Friday evening maintenance, 7–8 PM at the station. It's a low-key way to meet the crew and learn about brigade life before you apply.

### You Don't Have to Fight Fires to Join

The brigade runs on more than just crews on trucks. Every one of these roles keeps us operating, and none of them require you to be on the fireground:

- **Community engagement & education** — school visits, community stalls, helping neighbours prepare their properties.
- **Communications & radio** — running base radio, coordinating logistics during incidents.
- **Catering & logistics** — keeping crews fed, watered and equipped on long jobs and training days.
- **Administration & secretary support** — the paperwork that keeps a volunteer brigade running.
- **Training support** — helping run the courses that get new members qualified.

If any of that sounds more like you than firefighting, say so when you drop by — there's a role that fits.

### About the Brigade

The Bungendore Rural Fire Brigade is a volunteer brigade of the NSW Rural Fire Service, part of the **Southern Ranges** fire district, serving Bungendore and the surrounding Queanbeyan-Palerang region. Like every RFS brigade, we're entirely volunteer-run — the people responding to your call-out live in this community.

**Support us without joining:** running trucks, training and equipment costs money beyond what's centrally funded. If you'd rather support the brigade financially than as a volunteer, see the **Donate** link at the top of the page — every dollar stays with this brigade.
