### Where To Go If You've Left It Too Late

**A Neighbourhood Safer Place (NSP) is a place of last resort — not a plan, and not a guarantee of safety.** It exists for the moment your bushfire survival plan has failed and you have nowhere else left to go. If you can leave early, always leave early instead.

**Our nearest Neighbourhood Safer Place is Mick Sherd Oval, Bungendore.**

- Bungendore's main sporting oval, in the centre of town.
- [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mick+Sherd+Oval+Bungendore+NSW)

Sheltering at an NSP does not guarantee your safety. It offers a relatively cleared, open area away from the heaviest fuel loads — nothing more. Travelling to any NSP once a fire is close, especially along a single road, can itself be dangerous.

**Live outside Bungendore township, or want to check this is still current?** Neighbourhood Safer Places are reviewed and can be added, moved or removed by the NSW RFS. Always check the official, up-to-date list for the whole Queanbeyan-Palerang region:
